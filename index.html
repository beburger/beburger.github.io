About

I’m a graduate student at the University of Michigan–Flint, pursuing a M.S. in Computer Science and Information Systems with a concentration in Software Engineering. My work lies at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, and human–computer interaction (HCI), creating systems that are both technically robust and human-centered. Over the past two years, I have actively contributed to these areas, publishing research in leading HCI venues such as ACM CSCW and ACM CHI. Over the past few years, I’ve worked on projects ranging from AI-powered marching band formation analysis and wearable consent communication devices to XR-based interaction design. I enjoy combining practical engineering with experimentation, focusing on how technology can support accessibility, safety, and meaningful user experiences. My research interests include HCI, interactive systems, applied AI, and XR interfaces, and I’m passionate about learning new technologies, building creative solutions, and contributing to research that bridges people and computation.
